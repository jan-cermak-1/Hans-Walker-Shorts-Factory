What it does

Simple tool for slow creators

Designed for walking creators who want more content with less effort —
so you can spend more time outside.

Art. I

Batch Export

Generate up to 99 clips from one video. Start it, go for a walk, come back to finished clips.

Art. II

Direct Input

Click and type clips and duration directly — no fiddly arrows, no friction.

Art. III

Stays on your Mac

No cloud, no account, no uploads. Your footage is yours. Always.

Art. IV

Light & Dark

Follows macOS system appearance. Cream paper in daylight, dark ink at night.

Art. V

Auto Metadata

Title and tags set once — inscribed onto every clip automatically.

Art. VI

Live Progress

Watch the measure fill. Know the estimated time. Then go make a tea.

How to use

Three steps. Then walk.

I

Drop your walk video

Any long MP4 or MOV from your walk, hike, or commute. The longer the better.

II

Set clips & duration

How many shorts? How long each? Type it in directly. Done.

III

Export & enjoy

Hit Start Batch. Your clips are ready in your folder. Upload, post, walk again.
